# Combat advantage

- Adds +2 to the attack roll, regardless of whether it comes from flanking or a granted effect.
- Does not stack across sources.

## Flanking

- Two **allied** creatures flank a target when they are on opposite sides of it, both adjacent to it, and the target is **not** their ally.
- Each of those creatures has combat advantage against the target when it attacks.

## Granted combat advantage

- A power may grant combat advantage against a target for a duration defined by that power.
